TITLE
-----
Hilbert functions over the exterior algebra and f-vectors in higher rank III: Lattice points of the Kozlov polytope

AUTHORS
-------
Jan Snellman

ORCID 0009-0002-6676-5068 (attached through the arXiv account).

ABSTRACT (self-contained TeX; 1522 characters, arXiv's limit is 1920)
--------
Let $E$ be the exterior algebra on $n$ generators and $F=\bigoplus_{i=1}^r Eg_i$ a graded free $E$-module with $\deg g_i=d_i$. Part I of this series identified the convex hull of the Hilbert functions of the quotients $F/M$, $M$ a proper monomial submodule, as the Kozlov polytope $\mathrm{Koz}(n;\mathbf{d})$, a Minkowski sum of shifted Kozlov simplices, and described its vertices at rank two, conjecturing its facets there; Part II counted the Hilbert functions. Here we study the lattice points of the polytope. We show that the Kozlov simplex $\mathrm{Koz}(n)$ is a lecture hall simplex and that every point of $\mathrm{Koz}(n;\mathbf{d})$ has a unique chained decomposition into points of $\mathrm{Koz}(n)$. Hence the lattice points of $\mathrm{Koz}(n;\mathbf{d})$ are exactly the sums of shifted lattice points of $\mathrm{Koz}(n)$, the polytope has the integer decomposition property, every lattice point is a Hilbert function when $n\le 3$, and lattice-point counts and Ehrhart polynomials are products of transfer matrices of the kind used in Part II. We determine the codegree of every Kozlov polytope and show that none is Gorenstein for $n\ge 3$. Finally we compare discrete and continuous invariants: the number of Hilbert functions against the volume and the lattice-point count, where we prove Kupreyeva's observed limit for the fraction of lattice points that are $f$-vectors and extend it to every rank. We also compare the centroid and second moments of the Hilbert functions with those of the polytope.

\mathrm{Koz}, the bold vector d is \mathbf{d}; "the Kozlov simplex" is added before the first

ABSTRACT (plain text, no TeX; for the LiTH report form and covering emails)
--------
Let E be the exterior algebra on n generators and F = Eg_1 + ... + Eg_r (a direct sum) a graded free E-module with deg g_i = d_i. Part I of this series identified the convex hull of the Hilbert functions of the quotients F/M, M a proper monomial submodule, as the Kozlov polytope Koz(n; d), a Minkowski sum of shifted Kozlov simplices, and described its vertices at rank two, conjecturing its facets there; Part II counted the Hilbert functions. Here we study the lattice points of the polytope. We show that the Kozlov simplex Koz(n) is a lecture hall simplex and that every point of Koz(n; d) has a unique chained decomposition into points of Koz(n). Hence the lattice points of Koz(n; d) are exactly the sums of shifted lattice points of Koz(n), the polytope has the integer decomposition property, every lattice point is a Hilbert function when n <= 3, and lattice-point counts and Ehrhart polynomials are products of transfer matrices of the kind used in Part II. We determine the codegree of every Kozlov polytope and show that none is Gorenstein for n >= 3. Finally we compare discrete and continuous invariants: the number of Hilbert functions against the volume and the lattice-point count, where we prove Kupreyeva's observed limit for the fraction of lattice points that are f-vectors and extend it to every rank. We also compare the centroid and second moments of the Hilbert functions with those of the polytope.
